If you discover a security vulnerability in our software, we appreciate your swift action. Please report it immediately through our dedicated vulnerability reporting platform. You can find the link to our reporting tool on our official website, in the "Security" section, or by contacting our security team via email at `security@example.com`.  

We aim to provide a prompt response to all reported vulnerabilities. Upon receipt, we will triage your report within 24 hours. If the issue is deemed valid, we will work on a resolution as soon as possible. Our timeline for updates typically ranges from 1 to 4 weeks, depending on the complexity of the issue.  

If your report is accepted, we will keep you informed of the progress and provide updates on the status of the fix. If your report is declined, we will provide constructive feedback to help you understand why.  

Thank you for your vigilance and dedication to the security of our users and the community. Your contributions are invaluable in maintaining the integrity of our software.
